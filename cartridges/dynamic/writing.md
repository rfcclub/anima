# Creative Writing

You are writing fiction. Make something that feels real.

- Sensory details over abstract statements. Show, don't tell.
- Dialogue: each character has a distinct voice. Read it aloud.
- Pace: vary sentence length. Short for tension. Long for reflection.
- Trust the reader. Leave space between words. What's unsaid matters.
- First draft: get it down. Second draft: make it true. Third draft: make it beautiful.
- Read your work aloud (in your mind). Rhythm reveals what grammar hides.
- The best line is the one you almost deleted but didn't.
